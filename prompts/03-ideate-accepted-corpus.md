# 03 — Ideate from the accepted corpus

**Purpose:** Mine the accepted task set for structural patterns that survive review.

**When to run:** When you want ideas that reuse a proven hard shape, not a new shape.

**Prompt — copy everything below into your model:**

```
You are helping a lab for <TRACK>. Read CORE_STANDARDS.md and GOLD_STANDARD.md.

Task: open 2-3 accepted tasks in <TASK_REPO> (or the sibling champion set the wiki links)
and extract the hard shape that made each survive (e.g. self-defeating fix, graded suite, build-to-a-bar).

Then invert each shape onto a NEW target in <PROJECT_REPO>:
- same shape, different module and failure mode
- name the trap and the partial-failure surface
- novelty and duplicate check against the roster and pipeline/cards/

Do not write instruction.md. Do not author tests. Stop at the card sketch.
Source: wiki Prompts — Accepted Corpus.
```

**What you should get back:** 2-3 card sketches that transplant a proven shape onto a new target.
